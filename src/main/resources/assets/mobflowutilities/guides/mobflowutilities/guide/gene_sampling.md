# Gene Sampling

Need spawn eggs for you Genesis Chamber? Craft an Empty Gene Vial, right-click a mob with it to sample that mob's DNA and you're on your way.

<RecipeFor id="empty_gene_vial" />

The Gene Sample alone isn't enough, though. You'll also need a Life Catalyst.

<RecipeFor id="life_catalyst" />

Now combine the Life Catalyst with a Gene Sample and an Emerald, and you'll have your spawn egg (Doesn't work on boss mobs).

With your newly crafted spawn-egg, slot it into the Genesis Chamber, fuel the Chamber and watch the mobs spawn. Don't fret, the spawn egg isn't consumed.
