# Genesis Chamber

Spawns mobs from a spawn egg, similar to a vanilla spawner, while consuming fuel.

<RecipeFor id="genesis_chamber" />

The spawn rate can be increased using Speed Modules.

<RecipeFor id="speed_module" />

The spawn area can be configured through the Genesis Chamber's GUI and expanded using Radius Increase Modules.

<RecipeFor id="radius_increase_module" />

The Genesis Chamber can also be controlled with redstone for precise automation and control.

The Genesis Chamber follows vanilla mob-spawning rules regarding light levels. Hostile mobs will only spawn in complete darkness, while passive mobs require sufficient light.

## Genesis Infuser

<RecipeFor id="genesis_infuser" />

Need better, longer-lasting fuel for your Genesis Chamber? The Genesis Infuser allows you to infuse coal or charcoal with XP, creating Infused Coal and Infused Charcoal.

Simply pipe Liquid XP into the Infuser (from the XP Collector or another mod's XP source), supply it with coal or charcoal, and after 200 ticks it will produce an Infused Coal or Infused Charcoal.

The Infuser's processing speed can be increased using Speed Modules. Up to 10 modules can be installed, reducing processing time to as little as 2.5 seconds.

The Infuser supports simple bucket interactions. Right-click the block with an empty bucket to extract one bucket of Liquid XP, or use a bucket containing Liquid XP to insert it.
